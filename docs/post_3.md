🔥 **Watching the Continents Breathe Heat into Space** 🌍🛰️

I recently extracted and visualized Outgoing Longwave Radiation (OLR) data from the INSAT-3DR satellite. 

In the video below, **Red** means massive amounts of heat are escaping to space, and **Blue** means high, cold clouds are blocking it. 

Notice how the land masses suddenly "light up" bright red right around sunset? 

This is the **Diurnal Heating Cycle** in action! Due to thermal inertia, the ground absorbs solar energy all day and reaches its maximum temperature in the late afternoon. When it peaks, it acts like a giant planetary radiator, blasting thermal energy out into the vacuum of space. 

Meanwhile, the oceans barely change color at all thanks to water's massive heat capacity keeping sea surface temperatures stable.

It is amazing to go from parsing raw HDF5 arrays to watching the thermodynamic heartbeat of the planet!
