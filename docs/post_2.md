🌊 **Have you ever seen the sky break like an ocean wave?** 🛰️☁️

While analyzing raw multi-channel satellite data from INSAT-3D, I spotted this incredible ribbed "corduroy" pattern spanning the Indian Ocean. 

These are **Transverse Cirrus Bands**—massive gravity waves propagating through the atmosphere, driven by the intense wind shear of the subtropical jet stream. 

What makes this capture so special is decoding it across different satellite channels:
☀️ **Visible (VIS):** The low morning sun casts 3D shadows across the wave crests, highlighting their massive physical scale.
❄️ **Shortwave IR (SWIR):** Because ice absorbs SWIR (appearing dark), this confirms these aren't low-level water clouds, but a high-altitude, freezing cirrus system.

The wind shear here is so extreme that within these bands, **Kelvin-Helmholtz instability** is almost certainly occurring—meaning these clouds are physically rolling over on themselves in classic "surfer wave" spirals!

Fluid dynamics on a planetary scale. Absolutely incredible to watch unfold! 🌍
